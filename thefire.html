👁️ GOD'S OMNISCIENCE
He knew you before you existed. He sees every moment at once — past, present, future, all present to God. And He still sat you at the table.
  • He knew you before you existed. "Your eyes saw my unformed body; all the days ordained for me were written in your book before one of them came to be." — Psalm 139:16
    The Fire teaches: He was already writing your story before your first breath.
  • He knows everything — simultaneously. Not like watching a movie. He sees every moment at once. Past, present, future — all present to God.
    — CCC 600
  • He knows every card you'll ever be dealt. Every hand, every fold, every bluff, every river card — already known. And He still sat you at the table.
  • He knows the hairs on your head. Matthew 10:30. Not approximately. Exactly. That's how detailed His knowledge of you is.
  • Nothing is hidden from Him. "Everything is uncovered and laid bare before the eyes of Him to whom we must give account." — Hebrews 4:13
  • His knowledge doesn't erase your choice. This is the mystery. God knowing what you'll choose doesn't mean He chose for you. You're still in the hand. You still decide.
    — Romans 9
  • He wrote your name before the world began. "He chose us in Him before the foundation of the world." — Ephesians 1:4-5. The Book of Life isn't written at the end. It was written at the beginning.
  • Foreknowledge is not pre-programming. God knowing the outcome ≠ God forcing the outcome. A parent watching a child touch a stove knows what will happen. The child still chose.
  • This should terrify you — and comfort you. He knows your worst moment. Every sin. Every secret. And He still sent His Son. That's not surveillance. That's love with full information.
  • You cannot surprise God. Not with your failures. Not with your doubts. Not with your questions about omniscience at 2 AM. He's not disappointed. He's not scrambling. He knew.
📜 ROMANS 9 — THE HARDEST CHAPTER
"Not only that, but Rebekah's children were conceived by one man, our father Isaac. Yet, before the twins were born or had done anything good or bad — in order that God's purpose in election might stand: not by works but by Him who calls — she was told, 'The older will serve the younger.' Just as it is written: 'Jacob I loved, but Esau I hated.'"
— ROMANS 9:10-13
"One of you will say to me: 'Then why does God still blame us? For who is able to resist His will?' But who are you, a human being, to talk back to God? Shall what is formed say to the one who formed it, 'Why did you make me like this?'"
— ROMANS 9:19-20
This chapter is where most people walk away. Predestination. Election. God choosing before birth. It's the hardest teaching in Scripture. The Fire doesn't pretend it's easy. It sits with the weight of it — and finds that the weight is not crushing. It's humbling.
